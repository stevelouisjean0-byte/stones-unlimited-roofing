Before you call

What helps on the first call.

For a roof

  • Roughly how old it is. Even "the previous owners did it" narrows it usefully.
  • Where the sign of trouble is. A ceiling stain, tabs on the lawn, granules in the gutters, daylight in the attic.
  • Whether it only happens in weather. Wind-driven rain fails differently from a straight leak.
  • Any insurance claim in play. It changes the paperwork, not the workmanship.

For a build

  • The parcel. Address or parcel number is enough to start looking at access and setbacks.
  • How you reach it. Frontage width and whether anything large can turn around on site.
  • Where the plans are. Sketch, stock plan, architect drawings, or nothing yet — all fine.
  • What utilities exist. Or that you do not know — that is an answer too, and a normal one.
