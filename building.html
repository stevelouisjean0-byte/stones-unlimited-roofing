Talk about a build
The documented build

A flag lot that was not easy to access.

A flag lot is a parcel reached down a long narrow strip of frontage — the "pole" — opening into the buildable area behind other properties. They are cheaper per square foot for a reason: everything about them is harder.

Deliveries have to be staged rather than dumped. Concrete may not reach where it needs to reach. Utilities run further than a standard lot allows for. Survey, setbacks and turning radii all need solving before a single footing is poured.

That is the job the customer below describes, and the reason their review is the longest of the five.

Why it matters who roofs it

The builder and the roofer are the same person.

On most new houses the roof is subcontracted, priced to the plan, and never revisited by whoever drew it. Here the roofing side of the business is in the room while the house is still on paper.

01

Pitch chosen, not inherited

Pitch drives how a roof sheds water, how long the covering lasts, and how the house looks from the street. Deciding it with a roofer present is different from accepting whatever the elevation happened to show.

02

Fewer valleys, fewer leaks

Every valley and every penetration is a future leak that has not happened yet. Simplifying the roof plane at design stage costs nothing and removes failure points permanently.

03
